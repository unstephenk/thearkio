# The Ark — Brand Asset Guide

## Locked logo direction
The Ark uses a standalone geometric **A** mark. Do not use the earlier `TA` monogram.

## Primary usage
- **Header / navigation:** A mark + `The Ark` wordmark
- **Favicon / app icon / social avatar:** A mark only
- **Footer:** large `The Ark` editorial wordmark, supported by a small A mark
- **Open Graph / social cards:** A mark + The Ark + positioning statement

## Core palette
- Ink: `#090A09`
- Paper: `#F1EEE7`
- Bright paper: `#F7F4EE`
- White: `#FBF9F3`
- Accent gold: `#C8A46A` for brand assets; the site may retain its softer existing accent where appropriate

## Usage rules
1. Keep clear space around the A equal to roughly one crossbar height.
2. Do not stretch, rotate, skew, outline, or add shadows to the mark.
3. Use the light mark on dark backgrounds and the dark mark on light backgrounds.
4. Use the gold mark sparingly as an accent, not as the default logo color.
5. Do not recreate or use the discontinued `TA` monogram.
6. At very small sizes, use the A mark without the wordmark.
